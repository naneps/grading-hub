### Typography

**Heading Font** (Elegant, Modern)
- Option 1: **Inter Bold** (Google Fonts) - Clean, professional
- Option 2: **Poppins Bold** (Google Fonts) - Modern, friendly
- Option 3: **Playfair Display** (Google Fonts) - Elegant, luxury
- **Usage:** H1, H2, H3, navigation, CTA buttons

**Body Font** (Readable, Clean)
- Option 1: **Inter Regular** (Google Fonts) - Best for reading
- Option 2: **Poppins Regular** (Google Fonts) - Friendly tone
- Option 3: **Lato Regular** (Google Fonts) - Balanced

**Recommendation:** Inter (heading) + Inter (body) for consistency

**Font Sizes:**
- H1: 48px (desktop), 32px (mobile)
- H2: 36px (desktop), 24px (mobile)
- H3: 24px
- Body: 16px (default)
- Small: 14px (captions, fine print)

## 6. BRAND GUIDELINES (QUICK)

### Logo Usage
- ✅ Use full logo on light backgrounds
- ✅ Use reverse/white logo on dark backgrounds
- ✅ Minimum size: 40px width
- ✅ Always maintain clear space around logo
- ❌ Don't stretch, rotate, or change colors
- ❌ Don't outline or add effects

### Color Usage
- **Primary (#1F2937):** Headers, navigation, buttons
- **Accent (#F59E0B):** CTA buttons, highlights, hover states
- **Secondary (#06B6D4):** Secondary buttons, accents
- **Neutral (#F3F4F6):** Backgrounds, cards
- **Text:** Always #1F2937 on light, #F9FAFB on dark

### Typography Rules
- Headlines: Bold font, brand colors
- Body text: Regular font, #1F2937
- Emphasis: Use bold or color, not both
- Line length: 50-75 characters (optimal reading)

### Imagery
- Consistent before-after style
- Professional quality
- Diverse representation
- High contrast (preset-applied should stand out)

## 7. SOCIAL MEDIA PRESENCE

### Instagram Profile
- **Bio:** One line tagline + emoji
  - Example: "Presets for photographers ✨ | One-click color grading"
- **Link:** Website link
- **Highlight covers:** Free Presets, Before-Afters, Tips, Stories
- **Post frequency:** 2-3/week (if sustainable)
- **Content mix:**
  - 60% Before-afters (gallery showcases)
  - 20% Tips/Tutorials
  - 20% Behind-the-scenes / Personal

### TikTok (Optional, good for reach)
- 15-30 second reels: "Before-after preset application"
- Trending audio (if fits)
- Clear before-after comparison
- Call-to-action: "Link in bio"

### Email Newsletter
- **Brand colors:** Use primary + accent
- **Tone:** Friendly, helpful
- **Frequency:** Weekly or bi-weekly
- **Content:** 
  - Welcome sequence (3 emails)
  - Product announcements
  - Tips/tutorials
  - Unsubscribe always visible
